Based in Romania | Working with distributed teams

---

### What I Do
- Take ambiguous, unscoped problems and turn them into shipped, adopted systems — often with no roadmap handed to me
- Work directly with **clients and stakeholders** to translate business needs into product requirements, then build the thing myself
- Design **cloud-native architectures** with CI/CD and automation in mind
- Balance **engineering quality** with **commercial outcomes** — what it costs, what it's worth, whether it's the right bet
- Drive **user adoption**, not just deployment: training teams, measuring uptake, iterating on feedback

---

### Product Impact
A few outcomes I'm proud to have owned start-to-finish:
- Founded and independently run **Lotusly**, a multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform — vision, architecture, and code, with no team or roadmap to defer to
- **Business Processes** — architected and automated a full Odoo-powered marketplace back office (sales, invoicing, reconciliation, support) from a blank slate, with no existing playbook to follow
- **90%+ user adoption** within the first month of an ERP rollout, cutting manual process handoffs by **40%**
- Cut a freight quotation workflow from **3+ hours to 20 minutes**, and a separate quotation cycle by **50%**, by spotting the bottleneck unprompted and shipping the fix
